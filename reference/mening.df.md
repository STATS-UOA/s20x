# Monthly Notifications of Meningococcal Disease

This data shows the monthly number of notifications meningococcal
disease in New Zealand from January 1990 to December 2001.

## Format

A data frame with 144 observations on 3 variables.

- Month:

  Factor giving the month of notification.

- Year:

  Factor giving the year of notification.

- mening:

  Numeric number of notifications of meningococcal disease.
