# Fire Damage and Distance from the Fire Station

House damage and distance from the fire station, of 15 house fires. Data
collected by an insurance company for homes in a particular area.

## Format

A data frame with 15 observations on 3 variables.

- damage:

  Numeric Damage (1000s of dollars)

- distance:

  Numeric Distance from the fire station (miles)

- distance_km:

  Numeric Distance from the fire station (kilometres);
  `distance_km = distance * 1.60934`.
