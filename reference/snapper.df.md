# Snapper Weight Data

Weight and length measurements of 844 snapper ([Pagrus
auratus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australasian_snapper)) caught in
the Hauraki Gulf, near Auckland, New Zealand.

## Format

A data frame with 844 observations on 2 variables.

- len:

  Numeric fork length in centimetres. Fork length is measured from the
  tip of the snout to the fork of the tail.

- wgt:

  Numeric weight of the fish, in kilograms.

## Source

Russell Millar, University of Auckland.
